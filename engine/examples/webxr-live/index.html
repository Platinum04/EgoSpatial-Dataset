On Meta Quest 3 / 3S

  1. Run Space Setup once (Settings → Physical space) so the headset knows your table, couch, bed, door…
  2. Open this page in Quest Browser and press Start Mixed Reality. Allow spatial data when asked.
  3. Labels appear over your furniture and update as you turn: TABLE · LEFT · 1.8 m.
  4. Pinch (hands) or trigger (controllers): hear where everything is. Grip: place your own object where you look.

Also runs in Chrome on Android XR. Engine and research: github.com/Platinum04/EgoSpatial-Dataset
